FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

Is this a medical device?

No. The Deterica Kit is a demonstration kit and a training kit. It is not a medical device, and nothing on this site or in the app claims a therapeutic, rehabilitative, or diagnostic benefit.

Can I buy a prosthetic hand from you today?

No. The hand is not for sale yet. What is ready now is the Deterica Kit: the control, a desktop kit, and the app. If you want to follow the hand, write to us and we will keep you informed.

Who can use this control method?

It needs a preserved rotation of the residual limb, because that rotation is the input. A prosthetist is the right person to say whether a particular limb has it.

Why is the app useful with no kit?

Because the control model is the thing worth trying, and the app runs the same logic. A lever and two buttons on the screen behave as the kit behaves, so you can judge proportional control and grip selection before you own hardware.

Is the control logic open?

Not at present. The control library, the firmware, and the app are private. If you have a research or a teaching use for them, write to us and describe it.
