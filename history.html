The history

The first hand, and the one we build now

The line below reads from the bottom. It starts with the hand that was built, and it ends at the top, with the work of today.

  1. 2026 · now

    A new version of the prototype

    We are building the new version of the prototype. The app and the kit are the work of this year. The control logic runs already, and a recorded trace holds it to its behaviour tick by tick.

      The app

      Built, and it runs with no kit. It is free, and the App Store listing opens with the release.

    • In development

      The kit

      The mechanical part — the enclosure, the lever, and the moulds — is the work that sets the date.

    • In development

      The hand

      Work continues on the mechanics the control moves. It is not for sale yet.

  2. Before 2026

    The first hand was built, and it held a grip

    An earlier hand was designed, built, and tested. It ran on video, and it held four grips: pinch, point, forceps, and fist.

    The mechanics used cheap motors, and the design of the mechanics was weak. The concept held. The build did not, and that is the reason for the new version.
